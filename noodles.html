Shin Ramen

Spicy, rich flavour: Shin Ramyun is a popular Korean instant noodle known for its bold, spicy broth and rich flavour.

Quick and easy meal: It is simple and fast to prepare, making it a convenient choice when you are short on time.

Chewy noodles: The noodles have a satisfying chewy texture that pairs perfectly with the strong, spicy broth.

Bold, distinctive broth: Its spicy soup base gives Shin Ramyun a stronger and more intense flavour than many other instant noodles.

Easy to customise: Eggs, vegetables, cheese, or meat can be added to create a bigger, heartier, and more flavorful meal.

Buldak noodles

Popular Korean instant noodles: Buldak noodles are a well-known South Korean instant noodle brand made by Samyang Foods.

Meaning “fire chicken”: The name “Buldak” means “fire chicken,” referring to the noodles’ famously spicy flavour.

Thick, spicy sauce: Unlike regular instant noodles with soup, Buldak noodles are coated in a thick, fiery sauce that creates a bold and intense flavour.

Made famous by the Fire Noodle Challenge: Buldak noodles gained worldwide attention through the viral “Fire Noodle Challenge” on social media.

Many flavours available: Popular varieties include Carbonara, Cheese, and 2× Spicy, giving spicy-food lovers plenty of options to try.

2 Min Noodles

Quick and easy meal: 2 Minute Noodles are a convenient meal that can be prepared quickly when you are busy or hungry.

Ready in about two minutes: The noodles only take around two minutes to cook, making them a simple and time-saving option.

Variety of flavours: They are available in popular flavours such as chicken, beef, and vegetable.

Simple to prepare: Just boil the noodles, add the flavour packet, and they are ready to eat.

Easy to customise: Adding vegetables, eggs, or meat can make the noodles more filling and create a more complete meal.

MiGoreng

Quick and easy meal: Mi Goreng is a popular instant noodle meal that is simple and quick to prepare.

Sweet and savoury flavour: The noodles have a delicious combination of sweet, savoury, and slightly spicy flavours.

Dry, stir-fried style: Unlike soup-based instant noodles, Mi Goreng is mixed with sauces and seasonings after cooking, giving it a rich, dry texture.

Rich seasoning and sauces: The different sauce and seasoning packets are mixed through the cooked noodles to create their distinctive flavour.

Easy to customise: Mi Goreng can be enjoyed on its own or topped with fried egg, vegetables, or chicken for a bigger meal.

NissinCupNoodles

Quick and convenient meal: Nissin Cup Noodles are a popular instant noodle meal that is fast and easy to prepare.

Simple preparation: Just add hot water to the cup and wait a few minutes before the noodles are ready to eat.

Cook and eat from the same cup: The noodles are designed to be prepared and enjoyed directly from the cup, making them especially convenient.

Little clean-up: Since everything is cooked and eaten from the same container, there are very few dishes to wash afterwards.

Many flavours to choose from: Nissin Cup Noodles come in a variety of flavours, making them an easy choice for a quick meal at home, school, or work.
